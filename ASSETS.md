The visual target was generated with the supplied IUT campus references to preserve the warm red-brick, pointed-arch, palm-lined campus identity. The runtime uses the uploaded brick texture at `/manus-storage/iut-brick-texture_ec5cddf1.png` on building, gateway, bridge, and planter meshes. Other visual elements are procedural Babylon meshes so the arena remains lightweight and easy to extend.
