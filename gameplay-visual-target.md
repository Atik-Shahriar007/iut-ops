# IUT Ops Gameplay Visual Target

The supplied 1024×512 gameplay screenshot is the new in-game visual target. It depicts a bright daytime first-person view centered on a symmetrical red-brick water court.

## Composition

The camera faces straight down a narrow raised brick bridge. Long rectangular reflecting ponds run parallel on both sides, separated from the bridge by low red-brick parapets with repeated arched openings. A tall monumental pointed arch is centered on the horizon and acts as the main sightline anchor. Red-brick multi-story academic blocks frame both sides, with deep repeated pointed-arch windows and covered arcades. Palm rows with white-painted trunks and simple black linear lamps create a regular rhythm along both water edges and approach paths.

## Rendering direction

The scene is bright, readable, and daytime rather than dark tactical-night. Brick surfaces are warm red with visible variation, water is blue-green and reflective, paving is pink-red brick, lawns and palms are saturated green, and the sky is pale blue. Shadows are soft enough to preserve facade detail. The central arch should be the highest-contrast landmark.

## First-person presentation

The foreground weapon is a modern black assault rifle with a visible receiver, handguard, optic/red-dot sight, magazine, and gloved hands. It occupies the lower-right quadrant without blocking the central arch. The HUD remains minimal tactical overlay: directive top-left, sector/hostiles and radar top-right, crosshair centered, vitals bottom-left, controls low center, and ammo bottom-right.

## Acceptance criteria

- Starting view is stationary and aimed at the central bridge/arch axis.
- The central arch is visible ahead without requiring a demo orbit.
- Parallel water courts and brick parapets read clearly from first-person height.
- Daylight exposure prevents large black silhouettes from swallowing the architecture.
- Side buildings show repeated arches and palm/lamppost rhythm.
- The rifle silhouette is more like the target while preserving the existing shooting/reload loop.
