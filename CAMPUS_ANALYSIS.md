## Coherent spatial interpretation
The campus can be represented as a compact playable district organized around a central ceremonial/water courtyard. A large lawn or sports field sits beside the water complex. A major palm-lined avenue approaches the central buildings and gateway. Long red-brick residence blocks form secondary corridors around landscaped green areas. The perimeter has dense trees and an outer public road/urban edge.

## Signature visual language
- Dominant material: warm red/orange exposed brick.
- Repeated motif: pointed Islamic arches, arcades, cylindrical corner towers, brick parapets, and monumental gateways.
- Landscape: saturated green lawns, mature broadleaf canopy, rows of tall palms, trimmed shrubs, and planted beds.
- Hardscape: red/pink interlocking brick roads, bridges, plazas, and narrow brick sidewalks.
- Water: rectangular/irregular reflective ponds integrated into the central plaza, crossed by brick bridges.
- Lighting: slim dark modern lamp posts along roads and paths.
- Atmosphere: tropical South Asian campus, bright daylight or warm sunset, dense greenery and distant urban skyline.

## Recommended FPS map layout
1. Spawn at a palm-lined inner road near the campus edge.
2. Central objective zone at the monumental arch and water courtyard.
3. Flanking route through the large sports lawn.
4. Secondary combat/exploration route along residence blocks and landscaped footpaths.
5. Elevated/long sightlines around the central red-brick academic blocks.
6. Keep water areas as visual landmarks and traversal boundaries; bridges become natural chokepoints.

## Important design caveat
Use a stylized, compact interpretation inspired by the supplied references rather than claiming an exact survey-grade reconstruction. Avoid including identifiable private individuals from the reference images.
